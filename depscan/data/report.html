How scores work

Each package starts at 0. Every risk signal adds its listed weight and the total is capped at 100. 0–24 is low, 25–49 medium, 50–74 high and 75+ critical. Signals come from public data only: the PyPI and npm registries, the OSV.dev vulnerability database, download statistics and OpenSSF Scorecard via deps.dev. A score is a prompt to look closer, not a verdict.
